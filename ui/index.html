Never leaves the room

Continuous visual memory can only exist on-device.

Nobody will stream a live feed of their home, a hospital ward, a lab or a stockroom to a server. So a camera that remembers everything cannot be a cloud product. It has to run where the video is, and the video has to stay there.

Recall keeps video, names, questions and speech on the local link. The runtime has no cloud client and the UI has zero CDN. The internet cable can be pulled mid-demo and it keeps working, because nothing was going over it in the first place.

  • No cloud clientThe runtime cannot reach a server
  • Zero CDNEvery byte of the UI ships from the board
  • Frames kept 15 minutes, then deleted1 fps snapshots, nothing archived
  • People described by clothing only"Person in the blue shirt", never an identity

How it remembers

See, track, attribute, answer. One chip does all of it.

The vision-language model is never on the frame path. Stop it and tracking still runs; the memory keeps filling.

  1. 01 · See

    Segment every frame

    YOLO26 instance segmentation turns each frame into an inventory of objects with pixel masks. Every object gets a stable ID and a state.

    modelYOLO26 seg
    rateevery frame
    outputmasks + IDs
  2. 02 · Track

    Keep identity through time

    A tracker matches masks frame to frame so the mug that moves is still #3. Objects settle, go missing and come back without losing their ID.

    matchsame class, mask IoU > 0.30
    orcentroid < 5 % frame width
    stationaryafter 3 s
    missingafter 2 s
    reacquirewithin 60 s
  3. 03 · Attribute

    Whose hand was on it

    YOLO26 pose finds wrists at 10 fps. An object that moves or goes missing while a wrist was within 60 px of its mask in the last 1.5 s is a pickup by that person.

  4. 04 · Answer

    Grounded in evidence

    Events (appeared, moved, picked up, put down, missing, person entered or left with exit direction) land in SQLite. A question pulls a compact evidence JSON; Qwen3-VL-4B answers over it, citing event IDs.

    name≤ 5 words, with color
    peopleclothing only
    checktimestamp grounding
    voiceWhisper in, Piper out

The demo

Five questions, one table, no internet.

The whole demo happens on a table in front of the camera. The judges are the people in the memory.

  1. 1

    Take inventory

    QWhat's on the table?

    Five objects, each with a mask, a stable ID, a name from the VLM and a state. Recall reads them back with the snapshot.

  2. 2

    Move the mug off-frame

    QWhere's the red mug?

    The mug goes missing after 2 s. The answer names who moved it, when, and which way they went, with the snapshot from the moment of pickup.

  3. 3

    A judge picks up the laptop

    QWho took my laptop?

    Pose puts a wrist within 60 px of the laptop mask as it moves. The pickup is attributed to "the person in the blue shirt", never to a name or a face.

  4. 4
    Cable out

    Pull the Ethernet cable, ask again

    QWho took my laptop?

    Same answer, same latency. There is no cloud client to lose and no CDN to wait for. The link that matters is the one to the speaker.

  5. 5

    Replay the last ten minutes

    QWhat happened in the last ten minutes?

    A three-sentence summary over the event log: what appeared, what moved, who took what and where they went. Every sentence cites an event ID.

Where it goes

Anywhere things go missing and the video must not.

Homes

Keys, glasses, medication, packages. The things you lose every week, found by asking.

Where did I leave my glasses?

Hospitals

Custody of mobile equipment on a ward, without a patient's face ever leaving the building.

Who took the infusion pump?

Labs and workshops

Tool handoffs between people and benches, remembered without a sign-out sheet.

Who had the torque wrench last?

Retail back rooms

Stock that walks between the shelf, the cart and the door, with the moment it moved.

What left the stockroom after 6?
